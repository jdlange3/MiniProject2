# Reflection: ziotom78_healpix.jl

## Activity pattern
Healpix.jl has 742 commits from 29 authors and 11 gaps, so it is irregular, with long quiet periods in its early years and steadier work later (692 commits after the longest gap). Overall pattern classified as irregular.

## Interpreting the longest gap
The longest gap (2017-04 to 2017-12, 9 months) was the easiest to interpret. The maintainer's March 2017 commit says the Julia 0.6 update breaks 0.5 compatibility, so he avoided merging it, and the julia06 branch merged in Jan 2018.

## Likely reasons and recovery
A sole maintainer waiting on a language release before merging a breaking change. Recent history is mostly Dependabot, version bumps and docs.
