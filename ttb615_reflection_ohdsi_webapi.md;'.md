# Reflection: ohdsi_webapi

## Activity pattern
WebAPI has no gap of 3 or more months over 7,861 commits and 211 authors. Yearly commits rose to about 1,500-1,700 in 2018-2019, dropped to 252 in 2023, and recovered to 622 in 2024. Overall pattern classified as declining.

## Interpreting the longest gap
There is no gap to interpret, so the interpretation steps do not apply.

## Likely reasons and recovery
A long-running community project that never stopped. The decline is a slowdown and not an inactivity period, and recent history is mostly Dependabot updates with occasional feature work (Snowflake key-pair authentication).
